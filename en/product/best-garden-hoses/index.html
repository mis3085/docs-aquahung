Flat Garden Hoses for Reliable Outdoor Use
#

Hung Ta’s heavy-duty flat garden hoses are engineered to deliver exceptional durability and performance across a wide range of environments. Constructed from high-quality, eco-friendly materials and free from PVC inner tubes, these hoses are designed to withstand both extreme heat and cold. Unlike traditional hoses, they remain flexible and do not freeze or crack, even in temperatures below zero—a common issue with standard PVC hoses during winter months.

With a bursting pressure of 600 psi, these flat hoses are built to handle high water pressures without risk of bursting or leaking. The UV-resistant exterior ensures the hose maintains its integrity and functionality even with prolonged exposure to direct sunlight. Additionally, the robust construction resists leaks, abrasions, and punctures, making these hoses a dependable choice for demanding gardening and outdoor tasks.

Superior Flexibility and Handling
#

A key advantage of these flat hoses is their flexibility and ease of use. Unlike traditional round hoses, which can be cumbersome and difficult to maneuver, the flat design allows for easy rolling and compact storage. This makes it simple to move the hose around obstacles or sharp turns, and its lightweight nature reduces physical strain during use.

Efficient Water Flow and Self-Draining Design
#

Efficiency is central to the design of these hoses. The smooth interior minimizes friction and pressure loss, ensuring a steady and uninterrupted flow of water—ideal for tasks that require high water volume, such as filling pools or irrigating large gardens. Notably, models like No.3502249-5 feature a self-draining capability, allowing the hose to air-dry within 24 hours after use. This helps prevent the growth of bacteria or moss, keeping the hose clean and ready for its next use with minimal maintenance.

Versatility for Multiple Applications
#

These heavy-duty flat hoses are suitable for more than just gardening. Their high water flow capacity and resistance to tangling and kinking make them practical for agricultural use, transporting water over long distances, or navigating around multiple obstacles. Leak-proof connectors ensure secure and reliable connections, minimizing water waste. Whether watering your garden, washing your car, cleaning a yacht or pool, or even pet washing, these hoses offer a versatile and dependable solution.

For more information or to discuss your specific needs, contact us to learn how our heavy-duty flat garden hoses can enhance your outdoor tasks.
